Question

A policy trained only in the single-agent (SA) environment flies each of the 10 aircraft in the multi-agent (MA) environment. How many MA flights are clean with no MA training? Which failures remain, and which of them can a policy avoid?

A clean flight reaches its goal with 0 intrusions, 0 restricted-area entries and 0 sector exits.

Method

  • Policy. Lidar ray-token Transformer encoder with a PPO actor. It sees only its own observation: lidar, tokens for the nearest aircraft and zones, and its goal. The observation has the same format in SA and MA, so the policy cannot tell a scripted intruder from another agent.
  • Transfer. Each of the 10 MA aircraft runs its own copy of the same weights (decentralized, shared weights). There is no MA training and no change to the weights.
  • Floor. analysis/scenario_floor_ma.py replays the MA scenario generator with no simulator. It marks a flight as unavoidable when the start lies inside a restricted area (the environment counts the entry at reset) or the goal lies 5 km or more inside an area (5 km = capture radius). Check: every start-inside-area flight has an area event in the evaluation rows. The check passes on both seeds (0 violations, 10,000 of 10,000 rows matched).

Setup

  • Environment. Competition MA env: 10 agents, 5 restricted areas, no scripted intruders, 3000 s limit.
  • Split. Seed 7 = validation; the model was chosen on seed 7 only (clean %, then goal %). Seed 42 = leaderboard test seed, not used for any choice. 1000 episodes per seed = 10,000 aircraft flights. Deterministic policy.
  • Training (SA only). 2 M steps (sa_v2m_s0), then 2 M more from that model (sa_v2m_s0_c2). 64 workers, PPO lr 5e-4, γ 0.99, clip 0.2, rollout 512, 2 epochs, batch 128. Encoder: d_model 128, 3 layers, 4 heads, 120 lidar rays to 50 km, 10 aircraft tokens, 5 zone tokens, history 4.
  • Reward and v2 options (SA training). Preset los (loss of separation −0.5/s and −3 per event, area −0.3/s and −2 per entry, outside sector −0.2/s, time −0.015/s), heading −0.05, scale 0.05. Goal-relative action map (±90° from the goal bearing). Constraints-as-terminations (CaT): the first avoidable violation ends the training flight with −100. Margin costs −0.02 (7.5 NM aircraft margin, 10 km zone buffer).
  • Model seeds. One training seed. The ± values are the population std over flights, not over training seeds.

Limits

  • One training seed. The spread over training seeds is unknown.
  • The MA clean % uses our per-aircraft counting. The leaderboard's MA counting rule is not verified, so the leader comparison is not matched.
  • The floor uses a flat NM projection and straight-line geometry. It covers start and goal inside areas only. It does not count conflicts that no policy can solve; the replay check covers the start-inside-area case only.
  • The cause of the area crossings is not proven. The data shows where they occur (flights that need a detour), not why the policy enters the area.
  • The R1 values come from a screenshot, with one training seed and no result file in this repository. Its counting rules are not verified. The standard error of an R1 value is not known.
  • The videos are not a random sample of the evaluation. Fig. 6 shows four clean episodes. Fig. 7 shows three episodes that were chosen for their failures. The videos show where a failure occurs, not its cause.
  • Status EXPLORATORY: these results come from an evaluation, not from a planned, reported experiment.
